Transformers, introduced in 2017 in "Attention Is All You Need", replaced recurrent networks as the dominant sequence-modelling architecture. Instead of processing tokens one at a time, they use self-attention to relate every token to every other token in parallel, which makes training far faster on GPUs and captures long-range dependencies. Scaling transformers on large text corpora produced Large Language Models such as GPT, Claude and Gemini. These models are pre-trained to predict the next token, then fine-tuned with human feedback so they follow instructions. Despite strong results, they can hallucinate facts, are expensive to train and run, and inherit biases from their data. Current research focuses on efficiency, retrieval-augmented generation, longer context windows and reliable reasoning.
