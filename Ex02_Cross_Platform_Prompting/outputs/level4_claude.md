# Level 4 (Constraint) - Claude output

Transformers (attention-based neural networks), introduced in 2017, replaced recurrent networks (step-by-step sequence readers). Self-attention (comparing all tokens) works in parallel, speeding training and capturing long-range links. Scaled on large text and fine-tuned with human feedback, they became large language models. They can hallucinate, are costly, and inherit data biases; research targets efficiency, retrieval, longer context, reliable reasoning.
