# Preceding-question prompt — ChatGPT output

## Q1. Information needed
Order number, contact email, promised delivery date, current tracking status and delivery address confirmation through a secure support channel.

## Q2. Likely causes
Carrier delay, an incorrect or incomplete address, or a tracking update that has not synchronized with the carrier scan.

## Q3. Reassuring tone
Apologise for the inconvenience, acknowledge the lack of updates, avoid blaming the customer, and give a clear next step without promising an unverified delivery date.

## Reply
I’m sorry your order is three days late and that you were not kept informed. I can’t see your order from this chat, but I can help get it checked. Please contact our support team through the secure order page with your order number and checkout email. They can confirm the carrier status and whether the address needs attention. I won’t guess at a new delivery date until it is confirmed.
