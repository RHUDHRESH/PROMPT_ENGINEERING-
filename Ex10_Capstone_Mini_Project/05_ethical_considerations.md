# Ethical considerations
- **Safety:** wrong chemical or dose advice can harm people, crops and water; hard rules + expert-referral.
- **Hallucination:** ask questions when data is missing; show confidence; cite assumptions.
- **Bias & access:** language, literacy and connectivity; offer local-language, low-bandwidth mode.
- **Privacy:** farm location and yield data are sensitive; minimise, encrypt, get consent.
- **Accountability:** advice is decision support, not a replacement for agronomists.
- **Environmental impact:** prefer integrated pest management, avoid over-fertilisation.
