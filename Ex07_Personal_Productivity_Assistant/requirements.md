# Core requirements
1. Accept tasks in natural language; extract title, time, priority.
2. Organise by priority then time; give daily summary of pending items.
3. Schedule events; warn on overlaps; list free slots (09:00–18:00).
4. Suggest wellness tips (hydration, exercise, screen breaks) and adapt to feedback.
5. Persist preferences between runs (memory).
6. Simple command-line interface.
