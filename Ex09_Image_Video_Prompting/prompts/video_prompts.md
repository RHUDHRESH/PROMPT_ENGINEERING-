# Video prompt levels (same subject)
1. **Simple:** `A robot watering plants`
2. **Motion:** `A small brass robot waters tomato plants, water arcs from the can, leaves sway`
3. **Camera + timing:** `Slow dolly-in on a brass robot watering tomatoes in a foggy greenhouse; 8 seconds; light rays flicker; gentle ambient audio`
4. **Shot list:**
```
Shot 1 (0-3s): wide, greenhouse in fog, robot enters frame left.
Shot 2 (3-6s): close-up, water pours, droplets catch light, shallow DOF.
Shot 3 (6-8s): robot looks up at camera and smiles; pull-back.
Style: cinematic, warm, 24fps. Consistency: same robot design in all shots. Avoid: text, morphing hands.
```
