## Aim
Show how simple vs detailed vs structured prompts change image and video output.

## Tools / Apparatus
Built-in Codex image generation (four samples) and Grok Imagine via browser (one 6-second, 480p video sample). Further Grok generation required a paid plan.

## Procedure
1. Prepared four prompt levels for the same robot-gardener scene.
2. Generated one image for each level and saved them in `generated_images/`.
3. Scored image quality, adherence, coherence and style in `evaluation.md`.
4. Generated one video for level 1 in Grok Imagine and reviewed a visible frame. The first browser download did not complete. Levels 2–4 were blocked by the paid-plan gate; two public free demos were also unavailable.

## Observations
See `evaluation.md`. Image and video scores are single-reviewer judgments. Only video level 1 was generated; levels 2–4 are marked not run.

## Result
Four image outputs show stronger control after adding scene details and camera/composition terms. One 6-second video was generated and reviewed in Grok Imagine, but the MP4 download remained incomplete and is not in the repository. Levels 2–4 were not generated because the free generation limit led to a paid-plan gate.

## Conclusion
Detailed prompts improved visible subject and setting adherence in the image samples. The one reviewed video frame showed a garden and watering can with a robot arm but no full robot body. One clip cannot support a comparison across prompt levels; remaining video levels require a no-cost generator with working access.
