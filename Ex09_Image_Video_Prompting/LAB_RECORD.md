## Aim
Show how simple vs detailed vs structured prompts change image and video output.

## Tools / Apparatus
Built-in Codex image generation (four samples), Grok Imagine (one 6-second, 480p video), and Canva AI Video Clip (three video prompts; silent clips).

## Procedure
1. Prepared four prompt levels for the same robot-gardener scene.
2. Generated one image for each level and saved them in `generated_images/`.
3. Scored image quality, adherence, coherence and style in `evaluation.md`.
4. Generated level 1 in Grok Imagine. After Grok's paid-plan gate, generated levels 2–4 with Canva AI. Canva required audio to be disabled for level 3 and could not guarantee its requested 8-second duration. See `generated_videos/README.md` for prompts, service links and results.

## Observations
See `evaluation.md` and `generated_videos/README.md`. Image scores are single-reviewer judgments. All four video prompts now have a generated clip, but the clips are split across Grok and Canva, and the MP4 downloads are not in the repository.

## Result
Four image outputs show stronger control after adding scene details and camera/composition terms. Four video clips were generated across Grok and Canva AI, but the different services prevent a controlled level-by-level comparison. Canva clips are linked from the video-generation record. Their downloads were blocked by Edge, so no video binaries are in the repository. Levels 3 and 4 ran without audio, and the requested durations were not verifiable.

## Conclusion
Detailed prompts improved visible subject and setting adherence in the image samples. Video prompts 1–4 were generated, but levels 2–4 used Canva after Grok's paid gate, so model and prompt effects are confounded. The clips are available in the linked Canva account threads but could not be downloaded into this repository. A controlled comparison would require one service, matching settings, and accessible MP4 exports.
