# Exp-9: Exploring Prompting Techniques for Image and Video Generation

**Due:** 14 Sep 2026 · **Reference repo:** RaajaThilahar/Ex.No.9

## Goal
Show how simple vs detailed vs structured prompts change quality, coherence and style. Four image samples and four video clips were generated: one with Grok and three with Canva AI. The MP4 exports are not in the repository because Edge blocked Canva's download URL; the Canva results are linked in the video record.

- `prompts/image_prompts.md` — 4 levels of image prompts
- `prompts/video_prompts.md` — 4 levels of video prompts (Sora / Veo / Runway / Pika etc.)
- `generated_images/` — four outputs from one image tool (`level<N>_imagegen.png`)
- `generated_videos/` — video prompt outcomes, Canva AI result links and export limitations
- `evaluation.md` — image scores and video-generation record; video comparisons are limited because two services were used
