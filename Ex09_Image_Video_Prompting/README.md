# Exp-9: Exploring Prompting Techniques for Image and Video Generation

**Due:** 14 Sep 2026 · **Reference repo:** RaajaThilahar/Ex.No.9

## Goal
Show how simple vs detailed vs structured prompts change quality, coherence and style. Four image samples and one six-second Grok video sample were generated. The video MP4 download did not complete, and levels 2–4 were blocked by a paid-plan gate.

- `prompts/image_prompts.md` — 4 levels of image prompts
- `prompts/video_prompts.md` — 4 levels of video prompts (Sora / Veo / Runway / Pika etc.)
- `generated_images/` — four outputs from one image tool (`level<N>_imagegen.png`)
- `generated_videos/` — notes on the one browser-generated video and why its MP4 is not included
- `evaluation.md` — scored image comparison and one video sample, with remaining video levels marked not run
